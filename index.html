Welcome to IPHS 400: Frontiers in AI at Kenyon College. This upper-division course is a hands-on study of the AI frontier as it stands in late 2026: AI software engineering (AI-SWE), coordinating autonomous coding agents through a professional software development lifecycle. You build this competency through four mini-projects that stack on each other — your own development environment, agent configuration and custom Skills, agent harness/hook engineering, and a spec-driven SDLC capstone — before applying the whole toolchain to an original interdisciplinary research project.
